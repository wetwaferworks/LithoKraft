## The seed

A cardboard-box homage to Metal Gear Solid as a whole — a comical nod to Kojima's game, in LithoKraft's homage register (homage, never appropriation).

- **The box:** a box the player uses in connection with catching the **Grimlords** — the box JD had already been talking about for catching them.
- **The tap:** the box carries a **tapping sound that attracts them** — the Metal Gear knock-on-the-box lure, as a trapping mechanic.
- **The beat:** when a Grimlord hears the tap, it does the Metal Gear alert beat — an **exclamation point** pops — *"What was that noise?"* — and it comes to investigate, and is caught.

## Why it fits (registrar's note, not a design decision)

- A tap is a *signal* — in LithoKraft's language, a small knock that travels and gets answered. The lure is consistent with the engine's signal-first design.
- The exclamation point is maximally readable UI: one glyph carrying a full AI state (heard → suspicious → investigating), legible to series veterans and first-time players alike — "meet them at their level, teach by showing."
