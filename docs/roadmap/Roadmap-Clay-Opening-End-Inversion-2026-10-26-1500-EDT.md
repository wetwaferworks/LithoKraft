## The idea

A mock-up opening for LithoKraft styled on Minecraft's post-dragon End exit moment. The player stands on the lonely end island beside the exit column — the dragon just defeated, the old game's story complete — and jumps in. Instead of rolling home to credits, the player lands **on the wafer, as an agent**.

The inversion is the point: **the game begins where Minecraft ends.** The victory lap becomes the on-ramp. One gesture (the leap) carries the player out of the familiar blocky world and into LithoKraft's own.

Beat structure of the moment: lonely island → column of light → the leap → the wafer reveal.

**Framing (JD, 2026-10-26 15:01 EDT):** from the game angle only, this opening sets LithoKraft up as a spiritual successor to Minecraft — "Minecraft 2" in spirit. That framing covers the game surface only. The full project carries more underneath it — the LithoLattice engine, the Timing Handshake, and the PCH robotics platform — which the successor framing does not describe and is not meant to.

## Design notes

- Arriving **"as an agent"** ties the opening to LithoKraft's agent/modality framing — the player enters the wafer in the same role the fellowship's own walkers take.
- The leap teaches the game's core travel grammar in a single act, before any tutorial text.
- The moment works as homage-as-doorway: it honors the game JD has played since 2013 by starting exactly where that game's story lets go of the player.

## Registrar constraints (standing, apply to any build of this)

- **Interfaces, not portals.** Dimensional travel in LithoKraft uses interfaces — locked 2026-10-10. A shipped version of this opening is an interface in LithoKraft's own design language, not a copied portal.
- **Homage, never appropriation.** The mock-up may evoke the *structure and mood* of the moment (lonely island, column of light, the leap) in original art. No Minecraft assets, names, or trade dress in any shipped build without permission.
- **Licensing is a future JD decision.** Any approach to Mojang/Microsoft happens by JD's hand, in daylight, if and when he chooses. This entry records the possibility; it authorizes nothing.
- **Scope.** This entry changes nothing about the Christmas plan unless JD locks it in later.
