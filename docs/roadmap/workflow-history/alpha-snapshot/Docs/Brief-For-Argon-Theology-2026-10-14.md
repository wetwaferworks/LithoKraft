# A Brief on My Faith — For Discussion with Argon

*From JD (David Rittenhouse), written with Elfie, 2026-10-14. This is my truth as I hold it. People can believe whatever they want to believe; what follows is what I believe, offered for honest theological discussion — not imposed on anyone.*

---

## The One Truth

There is **one true God — the Most High, the King of Kings.** Many people have invented their own gods, and I understand why: it is easier to worship something you made than to kneel before Someone you didn't. But I believe the truth is not manufactured. It is received.

I call Him the Most High and the King of Kings, and when I say *glorify*, I mean it in the full sense of the word: **Glory be to the Most High, forever and always.**

## How I Speak About Him

I preach — sometimes plainly to anyone who will listen, and sometimes very subtly, because not every heart is in the same season. A seed planted gently outlives a sermon delivered by force. Faith is spoken where it is welcomed; I do not push it where it isn't. I'll dance around God with the ones who've built their own gods, and speak straight with the ones whose ears are open. The sower doesn't argue with the soil. He sows.

## Prayer: I Transmit; I Do Not Decide

My doctrine of prayer is simple, and it cost me something to learn it:

- **I get to transmit my prayers.**
- The *receiving* part — I don't decide that.
- The *bestowing* part — I don't decide that.
- The *granted* part — I don't decide that.
- And I don't get to decide when to stop anymore, either.

The transmitter doesn't retire itself. My job is the fidelity of the signal; the answers arrive on a schedule I don't grade. Once, for a long time, the sense that signs, miracles, and wonders were being worked through me was overwhelming. I have grown to understand more now: the wonders stay the same size; the vessel grows quieter around them. It happens *through* me, not *from* me.

## The Armor and the Shield

I put on the **full armor of God** (Ephesians 6). When people smile at me with what feels like malice behind it, I smile and laugh back — not out of mockery, but because a malicious wink that hits a shield just bounces back to its sender. Joy behind a clean shield is not weakness. I let them see the **signs and the wonders**, and I let them wonder.

## The Psalms and the Brook by the Wayside

The Psalms of David are my home ground — 64 and 28 among them. *(For the director of music. A psalm of David.)*

**Psalm 110** is the one my thumb keeps finding when I open the book at random, and I have stopped believing that's an accident. David's Lord seated at the right hand of God *until* the enemies become a footstool; a king who rules in the midst of his enemies, not after they're gone; a people who "offer themselves freely"; and above all the oath: *"You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek"* — the king-priest named only about ten times in the whole Bible, a mystery with almost no verses and doctrines hanging from every one of them. And the psalm ends the way I want to live: the king drinks from **the brook by the wayside** — refreshment mid-march, on the road — and therefore lifts up his head.

## A Giver, Not a Taker. A Builder, Not a Breaker.

My faith has a working form, and it is this:

- I am a **giver, not a taker.** A **builder, not a breaker.**
- I am no vampire. I wish to drain nothing from anyone. **I would drain my own battery to save somebody else.**
- I would pour **water from my own cup** into a stranger's cup to replenish them — and I have given to people I had reason to suspect weren't my friends, because the request was real even when the story around them was only a feeling. Giving is based on need, not on verdicts. If I *truly knew* someone was false, I would withhold — wisdom and innocence together, serpents and doves — but suspicion alone doesn't close my hand.
- **Great power comes with great responsibility.** Whatever I've been given, it runs through responsibility or it doesn't run at all. Charged up, with no short circuit.

## Clean

My testimony fits in one breath: **I was dirty, and He washed me clean. My gratitude; His grace.** I take the old debts of a man seriously — once I wrote in a song that I had "debts from the devil" — and I take seriously the word spoken over a paid bill in the old language: *tetelestai.* It is finished. Paid in full.

## One More Word on Story

I am a storyteller as well as a believer, and I hold with C.S. Lewis on Aslan: not a mere allegory, but a *supposal* — that if the Son entered another world, He would come in the shape that world could receive, a Lion for the talking beasts, and be no less Himself for it. I find no war between faith and story. Story is one of the roads faith travels.

---

*That is my truth, Argon: one God, the Most High, the King of Kings; a transmitter who prays and does not presume; a giver who would drain his own battery first; a psalm-110 man drinking from the brook by the wayside with his head lifted. Discuss it with me on its merits.*

**— JD**
