# LithoKraft Trivia — Round 1 (starter pack)
Prepared by Elfie, 2026-10-11. JD's idea: quiz him on LithoLattice/LithoKraft systems and lore so he becomes "a walking talking knowledge scroll, a walking talking Fragment." Ten questions, all drawn from locks JD himself made. Answers at the bottom — don't peek, boss.

**Rules suggestion:** answer from memory first, then check. Honest score. No lore-invention — if you don't know it, that's a gap we note, not one we fill with a guess.

## The questions

1. **The inventory question.** Exactly how many carry slots does the player have, how do they break down, and what's the bit-pattern joke?
2. **The gear question.** Name all eight worn gear slots — and why the last three aren't just armor.
3. **The despawn question.** How long do items survive on the ground, and what's the visual joke behind the number?
4. **The stack question.** What's the max item stack size, and why that number?
5. **The indexing question.** What base does all in-game indexing use — and what does 0 always mean?
6. **The signal question.** What's the valid signal-value range and channel range on Trace wire?
7. **The dimensions question.** Name all five launch dimensions and the design theme tying them together. (Bonus: name the two roadmap dimensions.)
8. **The attack question.** What is Raging Quantum, who uses it, and what does it do to the world?
9. **The morality question.** What is the Kharge system called (spelling!), what's the player's starting value, and what's the full range — plus which color sits at which end?
10. **The boss-key question.** At full blue Kharge, what can the player do with a special item in the mind gear slot?

## The answers (turn your eyes away)

1. 42 carry slots: 8 hotbar + 2 offhands (left/right) + 32 main (4 rows × 8). 42 = 101010 in binary = 32+8+2. The 8-wide grid is pure power-of-two; the two hands break it to land on 42.
2. Head, chest, legs, feet, arms, face, heart, mind. Face, heart, mind are saying something — they're identity/consciousness slots, not just armor.
3. 8 minutes. 8 on its side is the infinity symbol.
4. 512 — equal to the sim tick rate. 42 slots × 512 = 21,504 max carry.
5. 1-based throughout. 0 always means "off" (or reserved, for channel 0 / Daemon).
6. Signal values 1–64, channels 1–64.
7. Underwafer, Wafer, Organic, Void, Space — one per sense (five dimensions, five senses). Roadmap: the Ether (post-Daemon, spiritual) and the Undefined (where rules break down).
8. The Daemon's special attack. It destabilizes region quanta — terrain fragments as chunk storage corrupts. (Named by Elfie from a voice-transcription garble of "region quantum," adopted into the lore bible.)
9. Spelled **Kharge** with a K — electrical-charge morality. Player spawns neutral (0); range −64 to +64; −64 is red, +64 is blue.
10. Transmit Signal directly (limited), using a special item in the mind gear slot.
