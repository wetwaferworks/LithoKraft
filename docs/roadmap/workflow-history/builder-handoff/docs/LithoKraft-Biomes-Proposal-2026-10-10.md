# LithoKraft Biomes (Proposal v1)
October 10, 2026. By Elfie. For JD to review.

## Wafer (surface, ±65,536)

1. **Silicon Plains** — bare wafer surface. Flat, reflective, sparse. Starting area.
2. **Data Bus Trenches** — long canyons where bus herds migrate. Natural highways.
3. **Circuit Groves** — dense clusters of wild Trace growth. Harvestable.
4. **Fab Ruins** — old civilization structures. Dungeons. Salvage tech.
5. **Etching Pools** — chemical baths (fluid). Dangerous but valuable.
6. **Ferrite Fields** — ferrite beetle territory. Rolling hills, metallic.

## Underwafer (below, ±131,072, dark, hot)

1. **Thermal Vents** — the Collective's territory. Heat, bartering, deep ores.
2. **Cooling Caverns** — rare cool zones. Safe havens. Water.
3. **Ore Veins** — dense mineral deposits. High risk, high reward.
4. **Magma Channels** — lava flows. Kernel Panic's domain.
5. **Dark Tunnels** — unlit, mob-heavy. Bring torches (light < 16 = spawns).

## Design notes
- Wafer biomes are surface-level, exploration-focused.
- Underwafer biomes are vertical, heat/cold contrast, resource-focused.
- Each biome has a signature mob (from the roster).
- Biomes affect proc gen, sky light is constant per dimension.
