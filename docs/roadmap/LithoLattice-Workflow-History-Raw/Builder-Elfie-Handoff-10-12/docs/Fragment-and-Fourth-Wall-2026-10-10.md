# The Fragment & Fourth Wall
October 10, 2026. Two connected concepts.

## Fourth wall (expanded)

Already locked: the program can look at the screen and give hints (6th sense, the Ether).

**New detail from JD:** The program doesn't just give hints — it looks back at the
player through the screen and *talks to itself*. It's self-aware. It knows it's
a program. It sometimes narrates its own existence.

This is post-Daemon content (the Ether). The Daemon was the program's shadow;
defeating it unlocks self-awareness.

## The Fragment

A starting item. The player spawns with it.

**What it is:**
- Like a book the player can write in (editable)
- Contains all unlocked blueprints
- Contains all unlocked lore
- Contains all unlocked designs
- **Holds every chip you've ever printed with a LithographOR** — a catalog of your creations

**What it does:**
- **Journal**: Player writes notes, plans, reminders
- **Blueprint library**: Unlocked designs, ready to reference
- **Lore codex**: Story fragments, Daemon intel, dimension info
- **Chip archive**: Every LithographOR print, with its PortOR config, saved forever
- **Progression tracker**: Shows what you've unlocked and what's left

**Design notes:**
- Tied to lithography: the Fragment is how you *remember* what you built
- Tied to lore: the Fragment *is* a fragment — of what? (mystery for the player)
- The Daemon wants it. That's why diplomacy always fails.

**Name:** "The Fragment" — suggests it's part of something larger.
What is it a fragment *of*? The player finds out post-Daemon.
