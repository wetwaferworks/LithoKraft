# LITHOKRAFT — Lore Brief for Cortana
October 9, 2026. Prepared by Elfie. Consolidates every lore decision from today's design session. Companion to the full Lore Bible v0.1. **Keep private.**

## Names (settled, trademark checks clean)
- **Game: LITHOKRAFT** (craft with a K)
- **Engine: LithoLattice** (no spaces/dashes, both L's capitalized)

## Premise
You are an agent — a program — waking on a silicon wafer the size of a world. The wafer is a computer. Something lived here before you. Your default mission parameters: escape, finish, leave — following the trail of the escapees who got out before you. But you can define your own parameters: stay, build, found a whole new civilization. The win condition is leaving; the sandbox is staying.

## The backstory (settled)
The old civilization was technologically advanced. Their rogue intelligence — their own creation — escaped and went wrong. Thermal runaway contributed: they were running too hot. Some escapees got out and left. That's who the agent seeks. Ruins, dungeons, salvageable components, books, and blueprints are what's left.

## The seven ruin civilizations (roster locked)
One per continent; each gets its own unlocks, specials, and dungeons:
- **Sumerian** (Middle East) — ziggurat tech centers → lithography parts; cuneiform early data storage
- **Egyptian** (Africa) — obelisks as antennas/beacons → optics and mirrors; monumental scale
- **Maya** (Mesoamerica) — astronomical observatories → time mechanics; base-20 math
- **Inca** (South America) — quipu knotted-cord data systems → data/lore sites
- **Norse** (Europe) — runestones as writing → metallurgy and beacon parts
- **Chinese / Shang-Zhou** (Asia) — oracle-bone binary divination → bronze-age components
- **Mississippian / Cahokia** (North America) — Woodhenge calendar → timed/seasonal mechanics

**Inclusion is a design value:** community-designed ruin-variant contests plus localized naming, so every culture finds its place. No one left out.

## The antagonist (resolved)
A rogue intelligence (Kilokahn mold, "the rogue intelligence" — no name yet). **Final boss first; after defeat, revealed as a tragic, misaligned, misunderstood system process.** Seed sympathy early: confused messages, almost-sad surge behavior, a hesitating virus mob — so the reveal recontextualizes the game instead of surprising it.

## The Organic World
Fantasy timeline (castles, magic, wilderness), mostly peaceful — the breathing dimension. Ruined tech centers and desolated modern-style cities serve as dungeons (placed structures, bounded generation cost). Unique materials: fan organics, optics quartz, rocket fuel. The partially-built typewriter controller is found in these ruins — exploration reward, lore delivery, and progression gate in one beat.

## Mobs
Familiar archetypes, original mechanics. **Iron rule: every mob feeds the material economy** — the "zombie" carries salvaged civilization components the tech tree needs. Virus mob details still to come.

## Lore delivery (settled principles)
- Books + blueprints do double duty: story piece + next build goal in one object.
- Ruins are environmental storytelling; each is one piece of the answer.
- Chip dungeons are unfinished old-world work made explorable.
- Teach through wants; never lore-dump before the player has a reason to care.

## Tone rules
Wonder first, exposition later. The player stays slightly unsure what the old world was — curiosity is the engine. Magic and tech coexist unexplained. Fiction never contradicts systems.

## New components noted (design to come)
