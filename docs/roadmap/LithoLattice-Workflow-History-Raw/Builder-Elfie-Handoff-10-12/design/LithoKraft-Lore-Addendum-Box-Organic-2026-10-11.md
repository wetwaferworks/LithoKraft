## The Box (PROPOSED — JD, 2026-10-11)

A block that traps **Grimlores** and tames them as personal companions — the
LithoKraft answer to Minecraft's dog and cat. A tamed Grimlore follows the
player and helps in battles.

Canon verbs for the taming process — **reprogram, recompile, reconfigure**
(JD's words, locked as the game's vocabulary for this). You are not
befriending wildlife; you are rewriting hostile code.

Pack size: UNDECIDED (JD: "maybe won't have a limit" — left open).

## Power-curve placement (JD's framing, 2026-10-11)

JD named the design tension himself: too early and the player fields an army
of Grimlores and cheeses the Wafer and every other dimension; too late and it
never earns its slot against the mid-game bosses on the road to the endgame.
Levers on record for prototyping: gate the Box behind a mid-game milestone,
cap the pack (or price upkeep per Grimlore), and let tamed Grimlores fall off
in the late game so they're a mid-game power spike by design.

## Placed: the old rune fabs in the Organic (JD, 2026-10-11)

The Box is found in the **old rune fabs** of the **Organic**, on the player's
FIRST trip there. That arrival is now a designed beat:

- The player emerges in a **ruined fab** — the spot where the materials needed
  to build the etcher are found. Trees grow through the factory floor.
- **Limited time: half a day / 16 minutes.** After that, the player starts
  taking heavy damage. One trip, one clock, three grabs:
  1. the Box,
  2. the etcher materials,
  3. **organic wood** (a required material).
- The placement resolves half the power-curve worry by itself: the Box is
  *found* early, but fielding an army of Grimlores is a whole later game.

## Trap mechanic — DECIDED (JD, 2026-10-12; wording verified by JD same day)

1. The Box is charged by static, **via the Zap interaction**.
2. If the charge on the Box is high enough, it **attracts Grimlores**.
3. If a Grimlore interacts with a charged Box, it becomes **trapped**.
4. The Grimlore remains trapped for **X ticks** (balanced number NOT yet
   decided — JD's call).
5. After the hold, the Grimlore becomes **tamed** and accepts the player /
   agent's commands: **follow, attack, stay, sit, interact** — including
   item logistics: pass items to buffers, put items inside chests.

It is a lure, not a beat-down: no weakening the Grimlore first. Tamed
Grimlores are worker companions, not just combat pets.

## Command system — the Tone (JD, 2026-10-12)

Model: Ark Survival Evolved's dinosaur whistle commands, applied to
Grimlores — but NOT called a whistle. The command interface is **the
Tone**, a player / agent ability. The command set is still growing
(JD: "more commands to work on"). Decided so far:

- Modes: a tamed Grimlore can be set **passive** or **aggressive**.
- Once tamed, Grimlores are **fully commanded** by the player.
- Base commands: follow, attack, stay, sit, interact, item logistics
  (pass items to buffers, put items inside chests).

## Origin & the Daemon (JD, 2026-10-12)

- **All Grimlores are spawned via the Daemon.** They are his most prized
  creations — taming one is reprogramming his own work out from under him.
- Taming Grimlores grants **positive Kharge**.
- Taming also **aggresses the Daemon** against the player more.
- At the boss fight: fully positive Kharge improves the player's chances,
  but the Daemon is also more aggressive toward a positive being.
  Power and aggro scale together by design.

## Certificate Table & Certificate of Clarity — DECISION + PROPOSAL (2026-10-12)

JD decided: the **Certificate Table** is a craftable station, crafted on the
Wafer from Wafer materials; it crafts the **Certificate of Clarity** (which
fully reveals Demensors). JD delegated some material choices to Elfie.

- **Conversion block — DECIDED (JD)**: the hot flowing liquid is
  **phosphoric acid**. When phosphoric acid comes in contact with **water**,
  it turns into **silicon nitride blocks** — extremely hard to mine (the
  obsidian role). Real-world echo: hot phosphoric acid is the standard
  silicon-nitride etchant in fabs. (Supersedes Elfie's earlier molten
  silicon -> Silicon Ingot proposal.)
- **Certificate Table recipe — JD design in progress (2026-10-12 16:16 EDT)**,
  supersedes Elfie's earlier proposal:
  - **The Remnant** — a CRAFTABLE BOOK, the counterpart to the Fragment.
    Players craft a Remnant for the Certificate Table path.
    Refinement (JD, 2026-10-12 16:17 EDT): "The Remnant in the Stencil is
    the counterpart to the Book and the Quill" — the writing pair is
    **Remnant + Stencil** (Minecraft equivalence: Book and Quill).
  - **Silicon Nitride** — obtained (acid + water conversion), NOT crafted.
  - **A "diamond counterpart" material — DECIDED (JD, 2026-10-12 16:19
    EDT): the SAPPHIRE.** Deep in the Wafer at diamond depth, near where
    the phosphoric acid flows. Real-world echo: sapphire is a genuine
    substrate crystal (silicon-on-sapphire), gem-hard, blue. Candidate
    surfaced by Elfie from the real fab palette; chosen and locked by JD
    ("The sapphire, you found it. You get the credits."). Exact recipe
    counts/framing still open.
  - With those pieces decided, "then we'll have our certificate table."
- **Certificate of Clarity recipe (Elfie PROPOSAL, not locked)**: 1× Silicon
  Nitride, 1× Resonator Crystal, 1× Capacitor Can, crafted at the
  Certificate Table.

## Starting tool progression — DECIDED (JD, 2026-10-12)

Minecraft's opening loop, translated: punch the Wafer's surface trees ->
logs -> planks -> sticks -> make a **ChopOR**. The first ChopOR tier is the
tree material (the Wafer's wood equivalent, trace trunk). ChopOR tiers
beyond the first are NOT yet decided.
