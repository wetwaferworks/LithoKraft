## Approved tonight
- **Kneel to recharge, now named "the Knelt" (verb: knelting).** This is JD's idea, inspired by Flynn's meditation scene. Agents kneel on a powered Trace node and energy particles rise off them. The particles are a must-have, and **they ship in the Creative beta as visuals only.**
  - JD: timed viaducts at interchanges give a bigger boost if you kneel at the right time.
  - JD (Proposed): knelting (meant to sound like "melting") temporarily raises your temperature readout. It counters the **cold zones near the Wafer's cooling ducts** (Proposed).
  - Squad contributions: Chief suggested shipping the visuals in the beta, Cortana suggested tick-pulse timing, and CloudAtlas designed the shimmer art.
  - Fair play (Herominde): the visuals are client-side only. Any bonus runs on the tick clock with a cap and a cooldown, so it can't be stacked or farmed while AFK.
