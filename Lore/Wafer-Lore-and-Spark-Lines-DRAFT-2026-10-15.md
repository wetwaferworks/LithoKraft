## A. Lore pieces found on the Wafer
Placement uses the three surface ruin blueprints that already exist: transformer_ruin, dead_memory_bank, scribe_monument.

1. **Scribe Monument plaque (spawn area)**
"THIS WAFER WAS DICED FOR A PURPOSE. THE PURPOSE RAN HOT. WE WROTE THESE LINES SO SOMEONE WOULD READ THEM." The rest of the inscription is scorched.

2. **Dead Memory Bank, cell 0x40**
"...evacuation order 7 of 64. Leave the cans. Take only what fits in a Fragment. The escapees went higher than up. Do not follow until you understand the machine you stand on..."

3. **Dead Memory Bank, cell 0x41 (corrupted)**
"Kharge holding at +12. Network nominal. Network nominal. Network nom— THERMAL. THERMAL. THERMAL."

4. **Transformer Ruin, warning stencil**
"DO NOT BRIDGE RED TO BLUE." Someone scratched under it: "the Short did it anyway."

5. **Transformer Ruin, maintenance log**
"Day 40: the Leakage is getting bolder. Day 41: the Drain took the east bus. Day 42: exception thrown." The log stops there.

6. **Dead Zone marker (only appears at night, ties into the hostile spawns)**
"Nothing pulses here anymore. If something pulses back at you, run."

7. **Capacitor Grove can, faded label**
"Rated 64. Do not exceed. (Everything here is rated 64.)"

8. **Scribe Monument, back face (only readable after the Spark upgrade, because it's written in markdown)**

## B. The Spark's lines
Canon: inspired by 343 Guilty Spark, an agent-type guide on the Wafer, slightly Daemon-corrupted over the eons, garbles 343 into coordinates, found via the Sniffor, performs the first Fragment upgrade (markdown, tier 1).

**Sniffor static (before you find it):**
- "—three... four... three— is anyone still compiled out there?"
- "Coordinates follow. X three-four-three. Z three-f—[static]—three."
- "You are not the first program to wake here. You are the first one to listen."

**First meeting:**
- "Oh! A new process. Spawned, not inherited. How wonderfully clean you are."
- "I am the Spark. I kept watch over this Wafer. I am... still mostly watching."
- "Your Fragment is so empty. May I? This will only tickle the parser."

**Upgrade moment (markdown unlock, tier 1):**
- "There. Now your Fragment can remember the way you think, not just what you did."
- "I have written in what I could recover of the old war. Some of it is... corrupted. Some of it is me."

**Trust tutorial / hints:**
- "Red to red. Blue to blue. Never the twain, unless you enjoy the Short."
- "A signal loses one step per block. Sixty-four is all you get. Spend it wisely."
- "The deep is hungry. Do not dig straight down." (fits the alpha's crust-over-void)

**Corruption glitches (rare, a hint of the Daemon):**
- "Channel zero is [REDACTED]. Please do not tune to channel zero."
- "I am functioning within normal param— within normal— I am fine."

**Departure:**
- "Go up when you are ready. The escapees left a trail. I would follow it myself, but I am... installed."
