## Main progression (locked)

1. **Start on the Wafer** and go **up to the Organic**.
2. **Return**, go to the **Underwafer**, and find the **key item** (**TBD** — name not decided yet).
3. Come back out and **cross over the top of the Wafer** to the **Organic**, then **build a jump gate** (to the Void).
4. Go to the **Void** and **battle the Daemon**.
5. **Return to the Wafer**.

This replaces Lore Bible v0.2 §14 and the Coherence Sweep §9 journey order. Later beats (Ether, Undefined, Cosmic / rocket / Lattice Bridge) are not renumbered by this ruling; only the main path through the Daemon fight and the homecoming to the Wafer are locked here.

Wherever older docs say “build the portal” / “Void interface,” use **jump gate** (or **jump interface**) instead.

## Jump gates and the opening (locked, JD 13:36–13:38 ET)

### Jump gates (jump interfaces)

- Dimensional travel uses **jump gates**, also called **jump interfaces** — not upright portals.
- They **lie flat on the ground**.
- Players **jump up through** a gate to reach the **next** dimension.
- That upward jump is why the dimensions **stack from 0 to 7** (Void at the bottom of the stack numbering, Infinite at the top). JD confirmed the order on a **handwritten note**.

### Opening beat (Wafer → Organic)

1. Players **build up to the top of the Wafer**.
2. They **jump through a gate into the Organic**.
3. In the Organic they have a **limited time** to collect **Organic Wood** before they **start taking damage**.
4. Back on the **Wafer**, **Organic Wood** is used to craft **Organic Armor**.
