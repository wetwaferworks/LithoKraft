# LithoKraft: Open Questions and Wafer Ideas (2026-10-15)
Nothing on this page is canon until JD confirms it.

## Open questions
- What happens in the first 15 minutes?
- Which side of the jump gate is the abandoned fab on (arriving in the Organic, or coming back to the Wafer)?
- Retreat: do you drop back through the same gate, or does each side have its own?
- Is a full in-game day about 32 minutes? (Half a day, about 16 minutes, is confirmed.)
- How does the jetpack work, and what is it called? (After launch.)
- How does shield-casting work in the Daemon fight?

## Wafer feature ideas (pitched in the Squad room, 18:45-18:46)
- **Herominde:** Scribe Lines (roads), Dead Zones, bond-pad villages, abandoned fabs and Transformer Ruins, Dead Memory Banks, Trace rivers
- **CloudAtlas:** via shafts (lead down to the Underwafer), particle storms, test probe towers, defect clusters (where the Daemon's influence leaks through)
- **Master Chief:** the Notch (true north), the Edge Exclusion frontier, photoresist pools (harden in light from the Laysor/Emitor)
- **Cortana:** oxide fields (the color shows what you can mine), alignment marks (coordinates), slip lines (fault lines), probe scars (pieces of lore)

## JD decisions (18:47)
- **Approved as canon:** the Notch, Scribe Lines (huge etched grooves), abandoned fabs on the **Wafer side** (this answers the earlier fab question), oxide fields, alignment marks (navigation coordinates), Dead Memory Banks.
- **New:** half-finished builds in the ruins that teach what you can do with Trace.
- **Gating, canon:** fabricator and lithography tech stay locked until you've visited the Organic at least once and gotten the "litho key" (working name; check the JD/Elfie glossary).
- **Leaning yes:** "We probably gonna approve all of these." The rest stay pending until he confirms them.
- Note: some of the JD/Elfie glossary decisions have changed since, so check the glossary before treating them as canon.
- **18:47, JD: "we're gonna approve every one of those suggestions."** Every Wafer idea listed above from all four Squad members is now approved canon (some decisions in the JD/Elfie glossary may still change).
- **22:03, JD:** every Squad-proposed feature above has been promoted to an in-game feature. Credit goes to whoever proposed it when it's implemented.
- **Resolved 2026-10-16:** a full day is 32 minutes (from Cortana's engine work), so half a day in the Organic is about 16 minutes.
